# Privacy policy

*Last updated: September 28, 2026*

Cashout Dashboard doesn't collect, send or sell any of your data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
**The app has no internet permission, so Android won't let it connect to anything.**

## What the app stores, and where

Everything stays in the app's private storage on your phone:

- **Your shifts:** the numbers from each cashout slip and anything you type in.
- **Slip photos:** a copy of each photo you scan, kept so you can check a shift against its slip.
- **Your settings:** pay period, tip goal and display options.

The text on your slips is read **on the phone** by Google's ML Kit text recognition. ML Kit would normally send Google
anonymous usage statistics, but without internet access it can't send anything.

## Data only leaves the phone if you send it

- **Back up** and **Export spreadsheet (CSV)** create a file where you choose to save it, such as Google Drive.
  From then on, that file is covered by the storage service's privacy policy.
- **Android's own device backup:** if you've turned on Google backup for your phone, Android may include the app's
  data in it, the same as for other apps. The backup goes to your own Google account. The app never sends it anywhere itself.
- **Taking a photo** opens your phone's camera app, which saves the picture and hands it back to Cashout.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Delete all shifts** erases your shifts and photos. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
Files you backed up or exported yourself stay where you put them.
